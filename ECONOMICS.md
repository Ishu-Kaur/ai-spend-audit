# Unit Economics & Financial Projection

This document runs the unit economics and ARR projections for the AI Spend Audit tool as a lead-generation asset for Credex.

## 1. Converted Lead Value to Credex
When a high-savings lead books a consultation and converts into a Credex credit purchaser:
- Average startup purchases **$20,000** in AI credits annually through Credex.
- Credex operates on a **15% gross margin** on credit transactions.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) / Net Margin per converted customer:** $20,000 * 15% = **$3,000**.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Since our GTM strategy relies purely on organic developer distribution channels (Hacker News, Slack communities, and cold founder outreach):
- **Direct Ad Spend:** $0.
- **Operational Cost (Time spent on distribution):** Estimated at 15 hours of work equivalent to **$500** of initial setup and distribution effort.
- **Target CAC:** ~**$50** per converting customer, representing an extremely profitable LTV:CAC ratio of **60:1**.

## 3. Conversion Funnel to Profitability
To make this tool highly profitable, we project the following conversion funnel:
1. **Audit Completed:** 1,000 users.
2. **Consultation Booked (High-Savings leads):** 5% (50 users).
3. **Credit Purchase Completed:** 15% of booked consultations (7.5 users).
- **Total Revenue Margin:** 7.5 customers * $3,000 = **$22,500** from every 1,000 completed audits, easily offsetting our operational and database hosting costs.

## 4. Driving $1M ARR in 18 Months
To drive $1M in ARR ($83,333/month) within 18 months:
- At an average credit purchase margin of $3,000/year ($250/month) per customer, we need **333 active, recurring customers** on our platform.
- To acquire 333 customers at a total funnel conversion rate of 0.75% (from completed audit to active purchase), we need **44,400 completed audits** over 18 months.
- This translates to **~2,460 completed audits per month**, or **~80 audits per day** globally. This target is highly achievable if the tool goes viral on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and through organic developer word-of-mouth.
