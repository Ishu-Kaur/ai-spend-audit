## FAQ (5 Real Q&As)

### Q1: Do you require my actual credit card, invoice uploads, or login credentials?
**A1:** No. We never ask for password access, bank connections, or API keys. You simply input your current seat counts and tiers manually into our clean form interface to get your instant projection.

### Q2: How does the audit engine calculate my potential savings?
**A2:** The engine evaluates your input stack against verified retail pricing structures, identifies seat overlaps (such as paying for both Cursor and Copilot on a single developer seat), flags plan over-allocation (such as paying for Team tiers with too few users), and suggests highly equivalent cheaper alternatives.

### Q3: How is Credex associated with this free utility?
**A3:** This tool is fully sponsored by Credex. We sell high-quality, discounted AI infrastructure credits (Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise) sourced from companies that overforecasted their needs. If your audit reveals significant savings, Credex can directly help you capture those discounts.

### Q4: Is my startup's spend data shared publicly?
**A4:** Never. Your personal contact details (email and company name) are completely private and stored in our secure database. If you choose to share your report using our public shareable URL, all private identifiers are completely stripped out automatically.

### Q5: Are your recommended alternative tools equal in actual capability?
**A5:** Yes. Our recommendations are strictly defensible and usage-fit. For example, we do not simply say "use cheaper tools"; we look at your primary use case (e.g., coding vs. data analysis) to ensure any suggested downgrade or alternative tool matches or exceeds your daily workflow requirements.
