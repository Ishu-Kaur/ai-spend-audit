# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines the target audience, user acquisition strategies, and distribution channels for the AI Spend Audit tool.

## 1. Exact Target User
Our target user is the **CTO or Engineering Manager** of a Seed to Series A stage tech startup (team size of 10 to 50 developers). 

At this stage, the company is growing fast enough that they are buying licenses for Cursor, Copilot, and Claude on an ad-hoc basis, but they do not yet have a formal procurement or IT department tracking this software spend. They are the ones who get flagged by finance when the monthly AI tool bill spikes.

## 2. Pre-Intent Search and Scroll Behavior
Right before wanting this tool, our target user is typically:
- Googling search queries like:
  - *"Cursor Business vs Pro seat requirements"*
  - *"Is Claude Team plan worth it for 3 people"*
  - *"Managing GitHub Copilot seats in organizations"*
- Scrolling through discussions on:
  - **Hacker News:** Threads discussing the rising costs of LLM APIs vs flat-rate editor subscriptions.
  - **X (Twitter):** Posts complaining about developer productivity licenses overlaps.

## 3. Online Communities & Hangouts
- **Subreddits:** `r/SaaS`, `r/startups`, `r/devops`, and `r/nextjs`.
- **Developer Slack/Discord Groups:** local startup founder networks, local AWS user groups, and the Next.js Discord server.
- **X Lists:** Tech founder circles and engineering productivity lists.

## 4. First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
We will acquire our first 100 users through three highly focused organic channels:
1. **The "Show HN" Thread:** Post a highly detailed, non-promotional text breakdown on Hacker News explaining the "Hidden waste in startup AI stacks" and link to our free auditor.
2. **Cold DM Outreach:** Send 50 personalized, helpful DMs to early-stage CTOs on X/LinkedIn offering a brief audit of their seat licenses.
3. **Founder Communities:** Share our tool on Indie Hackers and private founder Slack groups as a free utility to help them shave $300-$1000/month off their overhead.

## 5. Unfair Distribution Channel
Our unfair advantage is **Credex's existing network**. Credex already has partnerships with incubators, accelerators, and startup portfolio networks. We can distribute this free auditor directly through partner newsletter lists as a value-add utility, gaining immediate trust and high-volume traffic.

## 6. Week-1 Traction Goals
- **Total Audits Initiated:** 250
- **Completed Audits (Email Captured):** 100
- **High-Savings Cases (> $500/mo):** 15
- **Consultations Booked with Credex:** 5
